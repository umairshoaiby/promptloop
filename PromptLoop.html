One free key unlocks every model (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral…). Get one at openrouter.ai/keys. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent only to OpenRouter — this file has no server.

Seed values are honest heuristics (seed), not benchmarked facts. Your 👍/👎 votes promote rules to learned (net +3 → medium confidence, +6 → high) or disable them (net −3 → disabled). Everything lives in your browser.
